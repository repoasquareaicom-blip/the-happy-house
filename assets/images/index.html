Transforming Classroom Wellbeing:

A Revolutionary Support Platform for Primary Schools

A wellbeing app designed to provide engaging activities and games rooted in neuroscience and behavioral science to support primaryschool-aged children. It is particularly beneficial for children affected by Autism, ADHD, or other conditions that may impact emotionalregulation and focus. The platform's activities are crafted to assist children in calming heightened states of mind, encouraging them toreturn to a relaxed and balanced state. Through a series of five interactive activities, children can engage in exercises that include questionsabout their current emotional state, gathered both before and during the activities. This valuable data is shared with teachers, offeringinsights to help guide the child’s emotional regulation and create a supportive classroom environment.
